Institutional Recruitment

A Reliable Bridge for European Deans & Registrars

Study Partners provides European state and private higher educational institutions with a reliable, compliant, and culturally integrated student pipeline from Africa.

1. Rigorous Credential Pre-Screening

We verify all secondary school leaving certificates (WAEC, NECO, KCSE, Baccalaureate) directly with African examination boards before dossiers reach your admissions committee.

2. 98% Visa & Arrival Compliance

Zero no-shows. Every student we represent is financially vetted, guided through consular procedures, and met at the airport by our staff.

3. Dedicated Pastoral & Campus Welfare

Our on-ground fellows assist international student deans with orientation, dormitory check-in, language integration, and health registration.
