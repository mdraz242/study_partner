Featured State Universities in Belarus

Minsk, Belarus

Belarusian State Medical University (BSMU)

Founded in 1921, BSMU is the foremost medical university in Eastern Europe. Trains over 2,000 international doctors with clinical rotations in 30+ affiliated state hospitals.

Featured Programs: General Medicine (MBBS), Dentistry, Pharmacy.
$4,600 - $4,900 / yr View Degrees
Minsk, Belarus

BSUIR (Informatics & Radioelectronics)

The technological epicenter of Eastern Europe. Specializes in Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Embedded Hardware, and Cyber Defense.

Featured Programs: Computer Science, AI Systems, Telecom Engineering.
$3,600 - $3,900 / yr View Degrees
Vitebsk, Belarus

Vitebsk State Medical University (VSMU)

Pioneer in English-medium medical education in Europe since 1981. Features modern anatomical theaters, dental simulation clinics, and a vibrant African students union.

Featured Programs: General Medicine, Stomatology, Preparatory Course.
$4,100 - $4,500 / yr View Degrees
